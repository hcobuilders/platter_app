Settings

Minimal, phase 1, per the roadmap: theme, contact info, one OAuth connection, and the three controlled vocabularies the rest of the app already depends on. The last batch of Step 1 — after this, all 40 screens across six batches are drawn.

Free-form tags on the project card popover, distinct from Flags (6.4) — tags describe the project itself, flags describe a bid requirement. Different vocabularies, deliberately not merged.

  1. 1Trades are the join key between packages and subs — editing here is low-frequency, high-consequence, so it lives in Settings rather than being editable inline anywhere else.
  1. 2Tags stay separate from flags on purpose. A flag drives bid-submission behavior (required attachments, addenda gating); a tag is descriptive metadata. Collapsing them into one system would make flags mean less.

What Batch 6 closes out. This is the last screen of Step 1 — 40 screens across six batches, drawn from one data model and one token file, with no screen contradicting an earlier locked decision. Every controlled vocabulary the app relies on (flags, trades, tags, package templates, and Batch 5's automation rules and custom columns) now shares one CRUD shape, established once in 5.5 and reused three more times here rather than reinvented. Two things stay open on purpose rather than guessed at: Q-15 (SharePoint site structure) blocks 6.3's second connection, and dark mode's toggle is visible but inert until a real phase-2 pass. Step 1 is done; Step 2 is owner approval of the full set before anything becomes a real build.
