ITB & sub-facing planroom

Priority #2 (D-07): the surface that turns a scoped package into invited bids. 4.1–4.2 are internal — estimator picks bidders and sends invites from inside the app's own chrome. 4.3–4.10 are the planroom itself, opened from a magic link with no account (D-10) — a deliberately separate register, not the internal shell with a new skin (R-04).

⚠Open question carried in, not resolved here. Field-level privacy/redaction (E-13→E-19) was never specced — see HANDOFF.md. This batch treats sub-facing visibility only at the package level (Q-09: siloed, invited scope vs. everything else) — screen 4.4's "other scopes collapsed" is that assumption, not a redaction system. No lock/mask affordance is drawn here that isn't backed by a locked decision.

What Batch 4 commits us to. The planroom is now a proven second visual register — paper surface, no dark shell, no account chrome — reused identically across eight screens without inventing a new pattern per screen. Addenda acknowledgment becomes a hard gate on quote submission (4.6→4.7), which the data model already supports via bid.addenda_acked[]. Parsed sub quotes reuse the exact provenance mechanic from intake and internal parsing — bid_line.source='parsed' is now demonstrated end-to-end, not just named in the schema. And the field-level privacy question stays explicitly open: 4.4's package-level lock is real and specced (Q-09), but nothing here should be read as a stand-in for the abandoned E-13→E-19 redaction model — Batch 5's bid tab is the next place that gap could start to matter, once a sub's actual price is on screen next to a plug.
