Network, Data & Tools

Every internal screen has carried three inert labels in the top nav since Batch 2 — Network, Data, Tools — sitting next to a real, working Settings link. Nobody has ever specified what they hold: no D-## decision, no E-## edit, no Q-## open question covers them anywhere in DECISION_LOG.md, and the real Step 3 build (currently far ahead of these wireframes) left them exactly as inert as Batch 2 drew them.

⚑This batch is a proposal, not a spec. With zero source material to work from, each of the three screens below is one plausible reading of its own label, grounded in data the real app already has (subs, bids, budgets, CSI codes, provenance). Treat every screen as a draft to edit or replace in Claude Design — not as a locked decision the way Batches 1–6 were once the owner approved them. Logged as Q-17 in the decision log rather than silently assumed.

What Batch 7 is and isn't. Unlike Batches 1–6, this one wasn't requested screen-by-screen against a stated job — it's a response to three labels that have sat unbuilt in the top nav since the app's earliest wireframe. Every screen stays deliberately tethered to data and mechanics the real Step 3 build already has (subs & bids, budget variance, CSI codes, the #70 $/sf math, the #37 provenance groundwork) rather than inventing new backend concepts sight-unseen. Nothing here is a locked decision — logged as Q-17 in DECISION_LOG.md — and every screen is meant to be opened in Claude Design and changed, not implemented as drawn.
