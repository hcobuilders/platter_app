01

The mark, revised

  • Same idea, softer geometry. Four cells laid flat, one lifted — the scope matrix with a package pulled off the surface. Corner radius went from 1.5 to 4.5 on a 64 grid, which reads as noticeably rounder without losing the grid logic.
  • All four cells now share the outline. Previously the lifted cell was fill-only; apricot against floral white is too low-contrast to survive on its own. Keeping the stroke on all four makes the geometry consistent and fixes the contrast — the offset and the fill do the work of distinguishing it.
  • Sunset gradient on the lifted cell, now anchored by the strong orange. E-27: the gradient's warm end moved from Apricot Cream to the deeper #FF9F1C — the same token used for focus rings and the active command bar — into grapefruit. Still the only place both warm hues appear together, which is what makes it read as the mark rather than as UI.
  • Still survives at 16px. Three outlined cells in an L, one warm cell offset up-right.

Palette

Your five colors, plus two derived neutrals. Semantics carry over unchanged from the previous revision — only the hues moved, and E-26 split the light surface in two.

Deep Cerulean#0E1F27Derived: cerulean at 11% lightness. Shell, nav, command bar.
Page#F3F5F6E-26: derived, cool. General work surfaces — dashboard, tables, project workspace.
Floral White#FEF9EFE-26: reserved for planroom and document-sheet surfaces only — the one place warmth still reads as paper.
Apricot Cream#FFCB77Washes and large surfaces. Selection lives one step up now — see Strong Orange.
Strong Orange#FF9F1CE-27: high-emphasis selection, focus rings, the active command bar, the logo's lifted cell.
Cerulean#227C9DStructure, links, primary data series
Light Sea Green#17C3B2Confirmed, complete, high confidence
Grapefruit#FE6D73Bond, mandatory, overdue, low confidence

The one thing this palette forces: three of your five colors are too light to be legible as text on a light surface. Apricot lands around 1.3:1 against it. So every hue carries a paired text shade used strictly for type and hairlines — the bright hue fills, the dark shade speaks. Re-audited under E-30 after the E-26 surface change: every shade still clears 4.5:1, comfortably.
